# FUTURE_DS_03 – Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 3**.

The objective of this project is to analyze marketing funnel performance, identify conversion drop-offs, compare marketing channels, and examine business-segment performance using **Power BI**.

The analysis focuses on the movement from **Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs) to Closed Deals**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the marketing funnel from qualified leads to closed deals.
- Calculate the MQL-to-closed-deal conversion rate.
- Identify the number of unconverted leads.
- Compare marketing channels based on lead volume, closed deals, and conversion rate.
- Analyze qualified-lead and closed-deal trends over time.
- Examine closed deals across different business segments.
- Analyze declared monthly revenue across business segments.
- Provide actionable recommendations based on the analysis.

## 💡 Key Insights

1. The dataset contains **8,000 qualified leads** and **842 closed deals**, resulting in an **MQL-to-closed-deal conversion rate of 10.5%**.

2. **7,158 qualified leads remained unconverted**, representing a substantial drop-off between qualified leads and closed deals.

3. Marketing channels show differences in **lead volume, closed-deal volume, and conversion rate**.

4. Closed-deal volume varies across different **business segments**.

5. The qualified-lead and closed-deal trend charts provide a view of funnel performance over the available time period.

6. Declared monthly revenue varies considerably across business segments.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Analyze the stages between qualified leads and closed deals to identify major points of funnel drop-off.

2. Evaluate marketing channels using both **conversion rate and closed-deal volume**, rather than lead volume alone.

3. Investigate differences between business segments to understand variations in closed-deal activity.

4. Continuously monitor qualified-lead and closed-deal trends to identify changes in funnel performance.

5. Compare converted and unconverted leads to identify characteristics associated with successful conversion.
